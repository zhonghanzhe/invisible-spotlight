# Invisible Spotlight
## Short Description
* An interactive dual-screen experience where your phone becomes a digital "gaze," revealing hidden order and chaos within a digital landscape. *

## Concept / Intent
* In our daily lives, many details go unnoticed until we focus on them. This project treats our attention as an "invisible spotlight."  When you hold your phone steady, the world makes sense. Geometric shapes (circles, squares, triangles) align into clean, mathematical structures, representing how our minds organize reality. When you shake the device, that mental order shatters. The shapes scatter and explode, symbolizing moments of stress or sensory overload where our cognitive structures collapse. As long as you are looking, the spotlight is on. You are the one defining the boundary between order and chaos. *

## Technology Used
* p5.js: Used for all the visual rendering and the "physics" of the exploding shapes. The "invisible bridge" that lets your phone talk to your computer with zero noticeable lag. It uses your phone's built-in Gyroscope and Accelerometer (the same tech used for racing games or GPS) to track your movements. *

## How to Play
* On your Computer: Open the link and click the "SCREEN" button. This is your main stage. *
* On your Phone: Open the same link and click the "PHONE" button. * 
* Note: If your phone asks for "Motion and Orientation" permissions, you must click "Allow" for the interaction to work. *
* Interact: Tilt gently: Watch the shapes flow smoothly across the screen as if you are moving a physical spotlight.*
* Shake hard: Watch the order disintegrate into chaos. *

## Requirements
* A web browser.*
* A phone.*
* An active internet connection for both devices.*
